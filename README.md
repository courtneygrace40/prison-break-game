# Prison Break Game

*It has been two years since your brother was arrested for the murder of your parents. The evidence seemed overwhelming, and the jury sentenced him to life in prison…*

*But you know the truth. Your brother didn’t kill your parents, and every private investigator you have hired has fallen short of freeing him.*

*You are left with two options:
a) let your brother spend the rest of his life in prison, or
b) break him out.*
